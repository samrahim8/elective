FAQ

Questions parents ask.

If your question isn't here, email hello@elective.school and we'll get back within a day.

What happens after I join the waitlist?
We email you the moment applications open for the next cohort. Waitlist members get a 72-hour first-access window before public applications, and founding-cohort pricing is locked in regardless of when you enroll.
What ages is this for?
High school students, grades 9 to 12 (roughly ages 14 to 18). We group sessions so older and younger students learn from each other rather than separating by grade.
Does my student need a business idea already?
No. Week one is built around finding a problem worth solving. Most students arrive without an idea and leave with a launched business.
Do they need coding experience?
No. We use AI tools to build websites, automations, and products without writing code from scratch. Curiosity matters more than prior experience.
What's the attendance policy?
Students are expected at all 8 sessions plus Demo Day. One absence is fine with notice; two or more makes it hard to keep up since each session builds on the last. Virtual office hours help catch up between weeks.
What if my student wants to withdraw?
Full refund up to two weeks before the cohort starts. After week one begins, the deposit is non-refundable but the balance can be refunded prorated through week two.
How involved are parents?
Parents are welcome at Demo Day and at a mid-cohort progress night in week five. Otherwise, the program runs student-to-mentor so they own the work.
Will my student actually make money?
Yes, that's the goal. Week five is built around getting a first paying customer. Most businesses earn between $50 and a few thousand by Demo Day, and the playbook keeps running after the cohort ends.
Is there financial aid?
Yes, on a case-by-case basis. Email hello@elective.school with a short note and we'll work something out. We don't want tuition to be the reason a great student misses out.
